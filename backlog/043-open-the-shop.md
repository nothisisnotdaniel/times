# 043 Open the shop

As the owner of Suite Pro, I want the shop open with a working Suite Pro product, so that people can buy it.

## What

Register the Gewerbe and tick Kleinunternehmer on the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung. Create the store at the provider Context#399 recommends, the Suite Pro product (€39, one year of updates) and the renewal (about €19), connected so a buyer gets a key that `times pro activate` accepts. Run one purchase in test mode and activate it. Put the buy link on the landing page. Until the shop is open you can sell by invoice and issue keys with the #39 tool.

## Why

Only you can do this: it needs your accounts, your tax details and the employer's approval (ReviewPack#1) first.
