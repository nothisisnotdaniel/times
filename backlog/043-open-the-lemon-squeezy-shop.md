# 043 Open the Lemon Squeezy shop

As the owner of Suite Pro, I want the shop open with a working licence-key product, so that people can buy it.

## What

Register the Gewerbe and tick Kleinunternehmer on the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung. Create the Lemon Squeezy store, the Suite Pro product (€39, licence keys valid one year, 3 activations) and the renewal (about €19). Run one purchase in test mode and activate it with `times pro activate`. Put the buy link on the landing page.

## Why

Only you can do this: it needs your accounts, your tax details and the employer's approval (ReviewPack#1) first.
