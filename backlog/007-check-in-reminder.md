# 007 Check-in reminder

As a Times user, I want a reminder when I've been working for a while and haven't booked my arrival, so that I don't have to reconstruct my start time later.

## What

When the computer has been in use for a while on a workday (a setting, for example since 9:30) and today has no arrival yet, show a system notification once, with arrival booked from it if the platform allows; not on absence days or public holidays. Works through the menu bar and tray app or a small background check, never by keeping the terminal app in the foreground. Builds on the menu bar and tray app and absence days.

## Why

Forgetting to book the arrival is the most common gap in time tracking, and guessing it afterwards makes the balance wrong. A single notification on real workdays (not absences or public holidays) catches it early without nagging.
